Minnesota high school soccer schedules are out and assignors will be filling games before you know it! As a reminder, the negotiated rate for high school varsity games in 2025 is $87, with no discounted rate for second games in a doubleheader. Rate for lower-level games is $70, also with no discounted rate for second game in a doubleheader.

For those of you interested in officiating high school this year, particularly the Metro East and Suburban East Conferences, I have created an online calendar for you to select games that work best for you and to help avoid repeat teams in your schedule. Please note that this will give me an indication of the games you would like, but it does not guarantee that you will get all games you select.

I know it seems early to be thinking fall high school sports, but as you know other assignors start early. Thanks for being proactive with me!

Instructions

How To Begin:

Enter your name at the top of this page.

Selecting Games

Please select the checkboxes of games you are interested in officiating for each day you are available. (NOTE: You may select more than one location for each day.) Use the "Next" and "Previous" buttons at the bottom of each page to navigate through August, September and October.

Sharing Your Selected Games:

After you have selected all games you are requesting, select "Save Availability to File" at the bottom of the page. Your file will download to your computer's Downloads folder "<your_name>.json". After you have located your file, please email it to gametimeassigning@gmail.com. This is not a file you will be able to open and view, as it is a file for scripting purposes on my end only.

Saving Your Progress

If you are unable to complete your selections in one sitting, you can use the "Save Availability to File" button to save your progress to a file named "<your_name>.json". To restore your progress, upload the file using the "Restore From File" button.
